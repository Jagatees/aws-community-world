## Marker behavior

New people and groups carry `isNew: true`. Old refresh flags are cleared. Earth, Minimal, Map, Gallery, Directory, and profile views show `NEW` on the relevant marker or portrait; mixed clusters show a badge while individual existing members remain unbadged. Portrait failures display an initials fallback so the badge stays visible. The existing New filter opens only the current additions.

The updater now detects stalled or incomplete pagination, validates identities, stages dataset writes until all requested scrapes succeed, retains unverified omissions, preserves usable portraits, and normalizes reviewed group aliases. Public profile IDs were checked for all three new Builders to avoid treating renamed accounts as new people.

Changed locations were also reconciled for nine existing Heroes and 45 existing Builders. Pins now follow the refreshed location labels instead of retaining coordinates from an older country or region. The completeness check allows the observed gap between the public source and retained records, while rejecting large drops and stalled pagination.
