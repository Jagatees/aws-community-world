| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| San Francisco | United States | Open | Approximate city pin |
| Berlin | Germany | Announced | Approximate city pin; venue unannounced |

San Francisco's visitor information lists 525 Market Street, 2nd Floor, with courtyard entry. Attendance requires an event registration; this is not a walk-in coworking listing. [AWS visitor information](https://builder.aws.com/connect/events/builder-loft)

AWS announced the other three locations on 18 August 2026. The source does not provide opening dates or venue addresses. These markers identify cities and are not directions to a venue. [AWS expansion announcement](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/in-the-works-aws-builder-lofts-in-berlin-hyderabad-and-sao-paulo/)

The data is in `src/data/builder-lofts.json`. Each record retains its source and verification date. Update the status, address, precision, and visitor link only when an official opening is confirmed. Separate Startup Lofts and temporary GenAI Lofts are outside this category. Builder Lofts are not added to the historical analytics totals because no comparable historical series is available.
