Explore AWS Community Days

AWS Community Days are conferences run by local community organizers. They typically bring cloud practitioners together for technical talks, workshops, and hands-on labs. Builder Center Events cover a wider range of gatherings, including smaller meetups and online sessions.

Browse the Community Days directory or check the official AWS Community Day listings. The AWS Community Day Singapore 2026 companion covers the venue and travel information for that edition.
