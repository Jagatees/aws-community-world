Use the directory to search by name or narrow your results by region and country. For a geographic view, open the Community Builders map and select a country marker to see its summary and member profiles. Country markers show approximate locations.

What is the AWS Community Builders program?

AWS Community Builders is a program for people who work with AWS and share what they learn through activities such as writing, speaking, and building projects. The AWS Community Builders program page explains membership, benefits, and how to apply.
